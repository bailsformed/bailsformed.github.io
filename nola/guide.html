💛 What the island is for

Magnolia Isle is a cozy game that walks with you from your first semester at Tulane all the way to your white coat. It does three real jobs:

  • Shows you one small next step, so the pre-med road never feels like a giant list.
  • Keeps your hours safe. Med school applications ask for the date, hours and a contact person for every activity. The Garden saves all of that as you go.
  • Tracks your classes and an estimated GPA, so you always know where you stand.

Along the way you level up, earn stickers, and your little character grows (a bow, a stethoscope, and one day a white coat 🥼).

This is a helper, not a rulebook. Class names and dates are common Tulane pre-med patterns. Your Pre-Health Advisor has the final word.

🌱 Your first visit

  1. Open the island. Pip (the little bird) introduces himself in 5 short pages.
  2. On the first page, type the name you want Pip to call you.
  3. Tap Next → through the pages, then Let's go! 🌸.
  4. Your first quest, Hi, I'm Pip!, opens by itself. Walk through it to see how quests work.

Missed something? Tap Aa (top right) → Replay Pip's hello.

🗺️ How quests work

Each quest is a short story you tap through, one page at a time. The dots at the top show where you are.

  1. What it is: Pip explains the quest.
  2. 💡 Why it matters
  3. The steps, one per page. Tap ✓ I did it! when you've done one (+10 XP). Not yet? Tap Not yet, skip →. You can come back later.
  4. 🐦 Pip's tip, a kind note, sometimes helpful links, and a My notes box for names, dates and little thoughts. Notes save as you type.
  5. Tap Finish quest 🌸 (+100 XP). Petals fall. 🌸

You can finish a quest even if you skipped some steps. Changed your mind? Open the quest, go to the last page and tap Mark not done.

Some steps send you to other parts of the island, like "Log one activity" (the Garden) or "Read MD vs DO" (the Mailbox). The game doesn't check those for you. Tap ✓ I did it! once you have.

🌷 Logging hours (the Garden)

Tap ＋ Log hours and fill in:

  • What did you do? Pick one: 👀 Shadow MD, 🦴 Shadow DO, 💗 Clinical job, 🩹 Clinical volunteer, 🙌 Service, 🔬 Research, ⭐ Leadership, or 💼 Other work.
  • How many hours? Use − and ＋ (half-hour steps, up to 24).
  • When? Defaults to today. Change it for older activities.
  • Where? and Who can vouch for it? Applications ask for a contact person, so fill this in now while you remember.
  • A little memory (optional). Future-you will use these for your personal statement.
  • 💛 Counts toward my Honors service hours. Turns on by itself for Service and Clinical volunteer. Switch it off if a log doesn't count for honors.

Tap Plant it 🌸. You get 2 XP per hour and a new flower in that activity's garden bed.

What the Garden shows

  • Goal rings: shadowing (≈50 hrs), patient care (≈300), community service (≈150), research (≈100) and leadership (≈100). These are common targets, not rules.
  • 💛 Honors helping hours: fills up to your 20-hour honors requirement.
  • Flower beds: one bed per type, one flower per log.
  • 📔 Garden diary: every log, newest first.

Delete a log: tap 🗑️, then tap Delete? within a couple of seconds.
Fix a log: there's no edit button. Delete it and log it again.

Take your hours with you: 📋 Copy for Sheets copies a table you can paste into cell A1 of Google Sheets or Excel. ⬇ Download saves a CSV file. You'll want this on application day.

📚 Classes and GPA (the Books)

Each book is a class. The shelf starts with common Tulane pre-med classes. Calculus I and Writing are already marked done as transfer credit.

  • Tap a book to set Not yet, Taking now (📖) or Done ✅, pick your final grade, change the credits, and add a school or note. Tap Save 💛.
  • Picking a grade marks the class Done for you. Tap the same grade again to clear it.
  • ＋ Add a class for anything else (your double major, electives). Pick its kind (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Math, Social science, Writing or Other) and credits. Classes you add can be removed. The starting ones can't.

At the top you'll see:

  • ⭐ Overall GPA (estimate): every done class with a letter grade, weighted by credits.
  • 🧪 Science GPA (estimate): biology, chemistry, physics and math classes only. Med schools call this your BCPM GPA.
  • 📚 Pre-med classes done: how many of the starting classes are finished.

P (pass) and W (withdrew) don't count toward the GPA. Med school applications recalculate GPA their own way, so think of these numbers as a friendly estimate.

📋 Copy classes for Sheets and ⬇ Download classes work just like the Garden's buttons.

☁️ How saving works

There's no save button. The island saves itself every time you change anything. Your progress can live in three places:

1. On your phone (always on)

Everything saves in the browser you're using (like Safari). It's instant and needs no internet. But it can disappear if you clear your browsing history or website data, use a private tab, switch browsers, or get a new phone. Safari can also tidy away data for websites you haven't opened in a while.

2. Your Village account (the safety net)

If you're logged in to the Family Village in the same browser, the island also saves to your Village account, about a second after each change.

  • The first time it connects, you'll see ☁ Your island now saves to your Village account too 🌸.
  • After that, the very bottom of the page says saves on this device + Nola's Village account. If it only says saves on this device, you're not connected.
It saves to whoever is logged in to the Village on that browser. Always play while logged in as yourself.

How to log in to the Village so your island saves

  1. In the same browser you play in, go to bailsformed.github.io/village/.
  2. Tap 👋 I've been here before. (First time ever? Use 🔑 I'm new here, or the invite link Bailey texted you.)
  3. Type the family code, your name and your 4-digit PIN, then tap Let me in.
  4. Go back to the island and reload the page. Check the bottom line says "+ Nola's Village account".

You stay logged in on that browser until you log out or clear its data.

📱 If you added the Village or the island to your Home Screen as an app icon, that icon keeps its own separate storage on iPhone. Log in to the Village in the same place you open the island: both in Safari, or both from Home Screen icons.

3. Backup codes (your just-in-case copy)

About once a week, Pip shows a little reminder. Tap Back up now (or Tomorrow to snooze it a day). You can also go to Aa → 💾 Keep your island safe any time:

  • ⬇ Download backup saves a file named like magnolia-backup-2026-11-13.json.
  • 📋 Copy backup code copies one long line of text. Paste it into your Notes app or email it to yourself. If your phone won't let the page copy, a box pops up with the code: press and hold to copy it, then tap ✓ I copied it.

To restore: open Aa, then either choose the file under Restore from a backup file, or paste the code into the box and tap Restore. You'll see ✅ Your island is back!

Restoring replaces everything on the island with the backup, and that becomes your new Village copy too. Use it only when you mean it.

When you play on more than one device

  • After a device has connected once, the newest save wins. Log hours on your laptop, open the island on your phone, and the phone picks up the laptop's progress.
  • The very first time a device connects, the bigger island wins, so a brand-new, empty phone can't wipe out your real progress.

📱 Moving to a new phone

The easy way (Village)

  1. On your old phone, open the island once while logged in to the Village, so the latest version is saved. (Making a backup code too is a nice extra.)
  2. On your new phone, go to bailsformed.github.io/village/ → I've been here before → family code, name, PIN.
  3. In the same browser, open bailsformed.github.io/nola/. Your island loads from your account. (If Pip's hello pops up first, just tap through it.)
  4. Check the bottom says "+ Nola's Village account". 🌸

The backup-code way

  1. Old phone: Aa → 📋 Copy backup code. Text or email it to yourself.
  2. New phone: open the island → Aa → paste the code in Or paste a backup code → Restore.

Forgot your PIN? Ask Bailey, who can reset it or send you a fresh invite link that logs you right in.

🍵 Pip's tips

  • Log hours the same day, with a contact name. Hunting down who supervised you three years ago is no fun.
  • Write a one-line memory with every log. These turn into your personal statement later.
  • Don't rush the seasons. You can peek at later seasons any time, but "one thing today" is plenty.
  • Enter grades at the end of each semester so your GPA estimate stays honest.
  • Use 🔊 Read to me on long cards when your eyes are tired.
  • Make a backup code once a month and keep it in your email, even with the Village connected.
  • Copy your Garden into Google Sheets once a semester. It's a second copy, and it's handy for your advisor.

❓ Questions and fixes

My island is empty! Where did everything go?

Don't panic, and don't start logging new things yet. Most likely you're in a different browser, a private tab, or your browser data was cleared.

  1. Log in to the Village in this browser (see above), then reload the island. Your Village copy should come back.
  2. No Village connection? Restore your latest backup code or file from Aa.
  3. Still stuck? Text Bailey before you do anything else.
The bottom of the page only says "saves on this device".

The island can't find a Village login in this browser. Log in to the Village in the same browser, then reload the island. If you use a Home Screen icon, see the note in How saving works.

I logged hours with the wrong number.

There's no edit button yet. In the Garden diary, tap 🗑️ → Delete?, then log it again correctly.

I finished a quest by accident.

Open it from the Journey, go to the last page, and tap Mark not done. Its XP goes away until you finish it again.

The 🔊 button doesn't show up or doesn't talk.

Read-aloud uses your phone's own voice. Check the phone isn't on silent and the volume is up. If the button is missing entirely, your browser doesn't support it. Try Safari or Chrome.

Can I use it on my laptop too?

Yes. Log in to the Village on the laptop's browser too. Both devices then share one island, and the newest save wins.

"Copy" didn't work.

Some phones block websites from copying. A Copy this box appears instead. Press and hold inside it, choose Select All, then Copy.

Can anyone else see my hours or grades?

Not other Village residents. Your island isn't shown anywhere in the Village. It's stored under your own account, and only your login can load it, so it can come back if your phone loses it.

What does "Start over" do?

It erases the whole island (it keeps your reading settings) on this device and in your Village copy. Make a backup first. It asks you to tap twice so it can't happen by accident.
