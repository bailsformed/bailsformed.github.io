👑 What this game is for

A beige kingdom has summoned you to restore its Lost Archive of Self. Under the fantasy (and the flirting), it's a real tool for your craft business:

  • The six rooms ask you about what you actually love: rabbit holes, "too weird to sell" stuff, obsessions, your taste, what you loved making before money got involved, and what you'd make if nobody could judge you.
  • Your answers become a Creative Identity Profile, the "you" part of your business.
  • The Mashup Forge smashes two or three of those loves onto something you can make (shirts, koozies, tumblers and more, by Cricut vinyl or sublimation).
  • You'll sketch a dream customer and the words she'd type into Etsy, then get a ready-made question to ask Claude about your real shop.

The whole point: your weird is the market. Stop sanding it down.

This page isn't listed anywhere on the site, and neither is the game. Keep both links to yourself.

🌀 Getting started

  1. Open the game. Tap Answer the summons ▸.
  2. The story plays one line at a time. Tap Continue ▸ to keep going.
  3. Varrick asks what to call you. Type your name (or a nickname) and tap Say it ▸.
  4. Finish the summoning. Then you're at the Archive, with six sealed doors.

There are three kinds of questions:

  • Buttons: tap one. Some have an "other" option that lets you type your own.
  • Chips: tap as many as fit, add your own in the box (press Enter or Add), then tap That's me ▸.
  • Typing: write your answer and tap Say it ▸. Management won't take a blank answer. "Even a weird one. ESPECIALLY a weird one."

🗝️ Every tab

The top shows your name, level and title, an XP bar, 🔥 Varrick's composure, lost, and your save status.

🗝️ The Archive

Home base. The six rooms show as SEALED, ◐ IN PROGRESS or ✔ RESTORED (tap to replay). The 🔥 Mashup Forge door stays locked until all six are restored.

📖 Creative Identity

Your profile, built from your answers, one section per room, with a one-line summary from Varrick at the top. 📋 Copy it or ⬇ Download as a text file to keep it, print it, or show someone.

💡 Idea Vault

Every idea you've forged, your dream customer, and the Oracle. Opens after the Archive is restored.

⚔️ Varrick

His composure meter, the Bother him button (he says something new each time, often about things you told him), his unlockable 🔥 Scenes, and 📓 Things he remembers about you.

🏆 Achievements

23 achievements, "awarded by Management, who is upset about every single one." Locked ones stay hidden until you earn them.

⚙️ Save & Settings

Your save status, backups, your name, and the "start over" button. See Saving your progress.

🔥 Forge, Vault and Oracle

The Mashup Forge

  1. Pick 2 or 3 things from your Creative Identity to smash together.
  2. Onto what? T-shirt, Koozie, Tumbler, Ornament, Hoodie, Mug, Car decal, Tote bag, Sticker or Hat. Then ✂️ Cricut vinyl or 🔥 Sublimation. Once you've picked two things, the Forge offers a few "sparks" to get you thinking.
  3. What's the design? A saying, a picture, a joke, all three.
  4. How excited are you? 1 to 5 hearts.
  5. Would you make this even if it never sold? Hell yes or Honestly? No.
  6. Tap 🔥 Forge it. It goes into your Idea Vault, and Varrick tells you what he thinks.

The Forge's one rule: if it doesn't make you laugh or make you feel something, it goes back in the fire.

Who else loves this? (your dream customer)

Forge 3 ideas and this unlocks under the Forge. Name her (like "Petty Pam"), pick her age, where she hangs out (Facebook groups, BookTok, church, craft fairs…), three things she'd type into Etsy's search bar, and why she's buying. Tap Save my dream customer. Those Etsy searches are your keywords. Use them in your listing titles.

The Idea Vault

Every idea with its hearts and whether you'd make it anyway. Tap 🗑 Toss it in the fire, then tap again to delete one.

🔮 Ask the Oracle

Once you have ideas, the Vault builds a question with your whole profile, your ideas and your dream customer already in it. Tap 📋 Copy the question, then Open Claude ↗, paste it in, and keep asking follow-ups about your real shop. (It works in ChatGPT too.)

💾 Saving your progress

There's no save button. Every answer saves by itself. Your game can live in three places, and the status at the top (also shown in ⚙️ Save & Settings) tells you which.

1. This phone (always on)

Instant and private. But it can vanish if you clear your browser history or website data, use a private tab, switch browsers or get a new phone. Safari can also clear data for websites you haven't opened in a while.

2. Your Family Village account (the safety net)

If you're logged in to the Family Village in the same browser, the game also saves to your Village account, a second or so after each change. The status then says ☁ Saved to [your name]'s Village account. If it says Saved on this device · log in to the Village to back it up, you're not backed up yet.

  1. In the same browser, go to bailsformed.github.io/village/.
  2. Tap 👋 I've been here before. (Never joined? Use the invite link your kid texted you.)
  3. Type the family code, your name, and your 4-digit PIN. Tap Let me in.
  4. Go back to the game and reload. Check the status says ☁.
It saves to whoever is logged in to the Village on that browser. If you see someone else's name, you're on their account. Log them out first (in the Village: open your profile, scroll down, 🚪 Log out on this device).
📱 If you put the Village on your Home Screen as an app, that icon has its own separate storage on iPhone. Log in to the Village in the same place you open the game (both in Safari is easiest).

3. Backup codes

In ⚙️ Save & Settings:

  • ⬇ Download a backup saves a file named like little-freak-backup-2026-11-13.json.
  • 📋 Copy backup code copies one long line of text. Paste it into your Notes or email it to yourself. If your phone blocks copying, a box pops up with the code to copy by hand.
  • Restore: paste a code into the box and tap Restore, or pick a backup file. You'll see "Restored. Varrick says welcome back."

Making your first backup earns an achievement. Management hates a woman with a contingency plan.

Erase everything wipes the game on this phone and in your Village account. Restoring a backup also replaces your Village copy. Download a backup first.

Phone and computer both?

Once a device has connected, the newest save wins. The very first time a device connects, the bigger save wins, so a brand-new, empty phone can't erase your progress.

📱 New phone

  1. On your old phone, open the game once while it says ☁, so the newest save is uploaded. (Copying a backup code too is extra-safe.)
  2. On the new phone, log in to the Village: I've been here before → family code, name, PIN.
  3. In the same browser, open the game link. Your kingdom loads. Check for ☁.
  4. No Village? Open the game → ⚙️ Save & Settings → paste your backup code → Restore.

Forgot your PIN? Ask your kid. They can reset it or send you a link that logs you right in.

💅 Tips

  • Answer like nobody's reading. Nobody is. The weird answers make the best products.
  • Pick the bold option. It moves the story and the composure meter. Beige gets you a pity achievement.
  • Do one room at a sitting. They're short, and the game remembers where you stopped.
  • Copy your Creative Identity when you finish. Print it and stick it above your Cricut.
  • Forge a lot. Ideas are free. Toss the two-heart ones. Make the ones you'd make anyway.
  • Use your dream customer's Etsy searches in your listing titles and tags.
  • Play after 11 p.m. at least once. You'll see.

❓ Help, something's wrong

Everything's gone! I'm back at "You have been summoned."

Don't start over yet. You're probably in a different browser or a private tab, or your browser data got cleared. Log in to the Village in this browser and reload the game. If that wasn't set up, restore your latest backup in ⚙️ Save & Settings. Still stuck? Call your kid.

The status never says ☁.

The game can't find a Village login in this browser. Log in to the Village in the same browser (see the Home Screen note above), then reload.

The Forge is locked.

All six rooms have to say ✔ RESTORED, and then you need to play the "Something is happening in the Archive…" ending. Look for that button on the Archive page.

"Who else loves this?" isn't showing up.

Forge 3 ideas first. It appears under the Forge after the third one.

I want to change an answer.

Replay that room from the Archive and answer differently. Your profile updates.

I hit Continue and nothing happened.

The button ignores taps for a split second right after you answer, so a double-tap can't skip a line. Wait a beat and tap again.

Can the rest of the family see this?

No. It isn't linked anywhere on the site or shown anywhere in the Village. Your save is stored under your own account, and only your login loads it.
