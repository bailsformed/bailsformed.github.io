👾 Open Gremlin Town

⚡ The 1-minute version

  1. Open bailsformed.github.io/kyla/ on your phone. Gnorbert introduces the Committee and asks your name, state, and favorite fandom.
  2. On 🏠 Home, the card on the right (or right under the town on your phone) is your one tiny thing. Do it, tap ✓ Did it! That's the whole game.
  3. Too big? Tap 🤏 Smaller please. Brain too loud? Tap 🫠 Brain too loud.
  4. Add every college you're curious about in 🏫 Colleges. Got paid? 🫘 Money → 💸 I got paid.
  5. Something needs a parent? 🆘 Grown-ups has the exact text to send them.
  6. Protect your progress: log in to the Family Village in the same browser. Then the town backs itself up. (More on that below.)

💖 What the town is for

Gremlin Town walks with you from right now (senior year) all the way through freshman year of college. It does four real jobs:

  • Shows you one tiny next step, so applying to college never feels like one giant terrifying list.
  • Tracks your colleges: deadlines, tours, applications, decisions, and how much each one would actually cost.
  • Budgets your paychecks from your new job into jars, so college money grows without you thinking about it.
  • Tells you when you need a grown-up (like for the FAFSA) and gives you the exact text to send them.

When you do well, the gremlins do tricks. They are dumb tricks. That's on purpose.

This is a helper, not a rulebook. Deadlines and rules are different at every college and in every state. When the game and your counselor or a college's website disagree, they win.

✨ Your first visit

  1. Gnorbert says hello. Tap …okay? 👀
  2. Type the name you want to be called (it starts as Kyla Bug), pick your state (that's how the game finds your state's college money), and your favorite anime or fandom (the gremlins will reference it). Tap That's me ✨
  3. Read the 3 rules. Tap LET'S GOOO 🚀. You get 10 starter snacks.
  4. Stick around for about 20 seconds. Someone may stop by. 👀

🏠 Home

The town

  • Tap any gremlin and they'll say something. Each one has a Thing.
  • The sky changes with the time of day.
  • The college on the hill starts as a cloud with a ?. As you finish quests it turns into a building, then a colorful one, and when you pick your school it gets a banner with your school's name on it.

Your one tiny thing

The game picks the next step for you: setup first, then the urgent stuff (deadlines and the FAFSA), then everything else in order.

  • ✓ Did it! checks off the step (+10 XP, +2 🍪).
  • 🤏 Smaller please gives you a teeny version, like "just open the website." Doing the tiny version earns +2 🍪 too.
  • ⏭ A different one shuffles to another quest. Skipping is allowed.
  • 📖 Open quest shows the whole quest.
  • 📋 What do I text them? shows up when the step needs a grown-up. It takes you to the Grown-ups tab.

This week

Seven little boxes for the last seven days: ✓ means you did something, 💤 means a rest day. Your 🔥 streak counts both. Rest days count.

🫠 Brain too loud

Moth gives you three choices:

  • 🤏 Give me ONE tiny thing: a super small version of your next step.
  • 🫂 Sit with me for 5 minutes: starts a 5-minute focus timer with the gremlins.
  • 💤 Not today. Rest day.: no guilt, and it still counts toward your streak. (Gnorbert will mention tomorrow, though.)

⏱️ Work with gremlins (focus timer)

Pick 5, 10, 15, or 25 minutes. The gremlins sit at tiny desks and "work" with you. This is called body doubling, and it really helps ADHD brains. You can hide the timer and keep using the app: a little ⏱️ pill in the corner shows the time left.

You earn 2 XP and 1 🍪 per minute. Stopping early still counts every full minute you did.

Alerts

  • 🎩 Gnorbert's Serious Hat shows up when a college is due in 14 days or less and you haven't marked it applied. A red dot shows on the 🏫 Colleges tab too.
  • If a deadline already passed, Moth leaves a gentle note. Many schools still take late or rolling applications, so check their site.

Tap a quest to open it. You'll see:

  • Why it matters (the yellow box).
  • The steps. Tap a step to check it off. Tap again to uncheck.
  • 👪 grown-up means that step needs a parent or adult.

The State aid quest (Arc 3) shows a button to look up your state's college grant once you've picked your state.

🏫 College Cards

Every college is a trading card. Tap ＋ New card (or the dashed card) to add one. Add every school you're even 1% curious about, including your local community college.

New card: +3 🍪. Every evolution: +5 🍪 and a trick. Epic and up get a shiny holo effect.

What's on a card

  • Vibe: up to 5 hearts for how much you like it.
  • Applying: how you apply (Common App or their site), deadline, app fee, fee waiver, SAT/ACT policy, the 🚀 I APPLIED box, and the decision.
  • Money: the Net Price Calculator estimate, and later your aid offer (cost of attendance, grants, loans, work-study).
  • 👑 THIS IS MY SCHOOL: only one card can have it. Watch the hill on the Home screen when you check it. 👀
  • Stamps: WAITLIST, DEFERRED, MINE ✨, or THEIR LOSS (for a no, because it is).

⚖️ Compare money

Shows every school with money info side by side: free money (grants), what you'd still owe, and the gap after loans. Grants are free. Loans are not. If a gap is big, that's a reason to ask the school for more aid (Arc 4), not a reason to panic.

🫘 Money (the Bean Bank)

💼 My job

Type where you work, your pay per hour, and about how many hours a week. Beans shows you:

  • About what you make per week and per month.
  • Take-home ≈ about 90% of that. (Social Security and Medicare take 7.65%, and income tax might take a little more or none. Your paystub has the real number.)
  • About how much goes in each jar every month, and how many months until your College Fund hits its goal.

Logging money

  • 💸 I got paid: type what actually landed in your account (the net pay on your paystub). Beans splits it into the jars. +3 🍪 (once a day).
  • 🛍️ I spent: amount, which jar, what for. No judgment. Logging it is the skill.
  • Tap a jar to rename it, change its emoji or goal, or 🔁 move beans to another jar (like if Fun Money goes negative).
  • Money log shows each month (‹ ›): what you got paid, spent, and put in the College Fund. ✕ deletes an entry.
The Bean Bank is a tracker. It doesn't connect to your bank. Your real money stays in your real account.

🆘 Grown-ups

Some things need a parent. That's not you failing; it's how it works. Each grown-up mission has:

  • 👀 See the text: a ready-made message.
  • 📋 Copy text: copies it so you can paste it into your texts. (Change anything in [brackets].)
  • 📨 I asked (+3 🍪) and ✅ It's done (+5 🍪). Asking is the hard part.

Missions: the 20-minute college talk, the FAFSA parent part, the CSS Profile, opening a bank account, finding your documents, health insurance info, a ride to a tour, the deposit, health forms, and your first tax return.

💜 No grown-up can help? explains your backup crew: your school counselor, a college's financial aid office (say "I have unusual circumstances with my parents"), fee waivers, and Bailey.

📜 Scripts: copy-paste emails for your counselor and for asking a teacher for a recommendation, a financial aid phone script, and a script for any scary phone call.

💋 Seraphine

She doesn't work here. She just shows up sometimes to tell you that you look cute, and then she leaves. Dramatically. Tap her to make her leave sooner.

  • She's more likely to show up after you finish things (and she always shows up when you pick your school).
  • Want more of her? ⚙️ → Seraphine visits → A LOT. Want none? Never (she'll be sad).
  • Need a compliment right now? Den → Snack Shop → Summon Seraphine (25 🍪).

☁️ How saving works

Gremlin Town saves in two places:

1. On your phone (automatic)

Everything saves the moment you do it. Nothing to tap. But phones sometimes clear website data (new phone, "clear history," or Safari cleaning up a site you haven't opened in a while), so…

2. Your Family Village account (the important one)

If you're logged in to the Family Village in the same browser, the town also saves to your account. The line under your level bar tells you which:

  • "Saved on this device · log in to the Village to back it up": phone only. Go log in!
  • "☁ Saved to Kyla's Village account" (or your Village name): backed up. 🎉

To log in to the Village the first time:

  1. Easiest: tap the invite link Bailey texts you. It opens the Village already logged in as you. Then set a 4-digit PIN when it asks, so you can log in on another phone later.
  2. Or: go to bailsformed.github.io/village/ → 🔑 I'm new here → type the family code (ask Bailey), your name, pick a face, and make a 4-digit PIN.
  3. Then open bailsformed.github.io/kyla/ in that same browser. Reload once if it still says "Saved on this device."
📱 iPhone tip: if you add the Village or the town to your Home Screen as an app icon, that icon has its own separate storage. Log in to the Village the same way you open the town: both in Safari, or both from Home Screen icons.

3. Backup file (just in case)

⚙️ → ⬇️ Download backup saves a file like gremlin-town-backup-2026-11-13.json. To restore it: ⚙️ → ⬆️ Restore backup and pick the file. Restoring replaces everything with the backup, so only use it when you mean it.

Playing on more than one device

  • After a device has connected once, the newest save wins.
  • The very first time a device connects, the bigger save wins, so a brand-new, empty phone can't wipe out your real progress.

📱 Moving to a new phone

  1. On your old phone, open the town once while logged in to the Village, so the newest version is saved.
  2. On your new phone, go to bailsformed.github.io/village/ → 👋 I've been here before → family code, your name, your PIN.
  3. In the same browser, open bailsformed.github.io/kyla/. Your town loads from your account. (If Gnorbert's intro pops up first, it closes itself once your save loads. If not, just tap through it.)
  4. Check the line under your level says "☁ Saved to … Village account". 👾

❓ Questions and fixes

It says "Saved on this device" even though I logged in to the Village.

The town can't see a Village login in this browser. Log in to the Village in the same browser you're using for the town, then reload the town. If you use a Home Screen icon, see the iPhone tip in How saving works.

I forgot my Village PIN.

Text Bailey. Bailey can reset it or send you a fresh invite link.

I checked a step by accident.

Open the quest and tap the step again to uncheck it. If the whole quest finished, tap Mark not done.

I logged a paycheck or purchase wrong.

In 🫘 Money → Money log, find it (use ‹ › to change months), tap ✕, and log it again.

A jar went negative.

Tap a jar that has money in it → 🔁 Move beans → move some into the negative jar. Beans prefers you borrow from Fun Money, never from the College Fund.

"Copy text" didn't work.

Some phones block websites from copying. Tap 👀 See the text, press and hold the text, choose Select All, then Copy.

The tricks / animations are too much.

⚙️ → Calm mode. Tricks turn into a short message instead.

No sound.

Check that your phone isn't on silent and that ⚙️ → Sound is On. Sounds start after you tap something on the page.

Can anyone else see my colleges, money, or notes?

No. Nothing from Gremlin Town shows up in the Village. It's stored under your own account and only your login can load it.

A deadline in the game doesn't match the college's website.

The website wins. Update the date on the College Card.
