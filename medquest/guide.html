⚔️ Quick start

  1. Open bailsformed.github.io/medquest/. The Sage gives a 5-page intro, then opens your first quest.
  2. On the 🗺 World Map, the side panel shows your current chapter's quests. Tap one, check off steps, then ✓ Complete quest.

  4. Look at the save status next to the ⚙︎ button. You want ☁ Saved to Bailey's Village account.
  5. Do the weekly backup when the Sage asks. Two minutes, months of logging protected.

📜 What MedQuest is for

MedQuest turns the real path to medical school into a cozy pixel RPG: finish your bachelor's at SUNY New Paltz while collecting everything med schools look for (grades, the MCAT, shadowing, clinical hours, service, and letters).

  • 52 quests in 8 chapters explain what each step is, why it matters, and exactly what to do.
  • The Adventure Journal is your official record of hours, ready to copy into AMCAS and AACOMAS later.
  • The Spellbook tracks every course and estimates your cumulative and BCPM GPA.

The route is Straight Through: apply May 2028, start med school in 2029, with a checkpoint (The Fork in the Road, Jan 2028) where you can choose a gap year instead.

Dates marked ≈ are estimates from last cycle's pattern. Always confirm with AAMC, AACOM, New Paltz and MSAR.

🧭 The top bar

  • Your portrait: tap it to customize your hero (name, hair, skin, shirt colors).
  • Name, LV and title, plus the XP bar ("N XP to level X").
  • ⏳ Countdown to the next key date.
  • Save status: where your progress is being saved (see How saving works).
  • 🔊 / 🔈 turns sound on or off. ⚙︎ opens Save & settings.

🗺 Every tab

🗺 World Map

A pixel map with one stop per chapter. Your current chapter has a gold marker, finished ones get a green ✓. Tap any stop to see its quests in the side panel, with a button for the next one to do. The 📅 Key dates list underneath has deadlines and estimates.

📜 Quest Log

Every quest in every chapter in one list, with step progress and XP for each. Tick Hide completed to see only what's left.

📓 Adventure Journal

Hour meters and the form for logging hours. See Logging hours.

📘 Spellbook

Your courses and GPA estimates. See Courses and GPA.

🏆 Trophy Room

35 trophies. Locked ones show what unlocks them. Unlocked ones show the date.

🦉 Codex

The Sage's glossary: MD vs DO, AMCAS, AACOMAS, BCPM, MSAR, secondaries, committee letters and more. Look things up here when someone at school uses jargon.

Inside a quest

  • Chips show the chapter, when to do it, and its XP (40 to 300, bigger for boss fights).
  • ✦ Why it matters, ✦ Your steps (tap each to check it off, +10 XP; tap again to uncheck), the Sage's tip, and ✦ Links where useful.
  • ✦ Your notes: names, dates, what you learned. Saves as you type.
  • ✓ Complete quest gives the quest's XP. ↺ Mark not done undoes it.

Finish the last quest in a chapter and you get a ✨ CHAPTER COMPLETE ✨ banner.

The Fork in the Road (Jan 2028)

A checkpoint quest in Chapter 4: are your MCAT courses done, practice scores near goal, roughly 150+ clinical and 30+ shadowing hours? If yes, keep going straight through. If not, choose the gap year (graduate 2029, apply May 2029, start 2030). The quests stay the same either way. Only your calendar shifts, so treat the dates in later quests as one year later if you choose the gap year.

📓 Logging hours (Adventure Journal)

Fill in the ➕ Log an adventure form and tap Log it! (+2 XP per hour):

  • Date and Hours.
  • Type: Shadowing · MD, Shadowing · DO, Clinical · Paid job, Clinical · Volunteer, Non-clinical service, Research, Leadership, Work (non-clinical).
  • Place / organization, Contact (name / email) and a memorable moment / reflection.

Use the Contact field for shadowing. The Shadow Squad trophy and the "N physicians" count on the meter count different contact names.

Past jobs count. Log your HR, management and tech years as Leadership or Work with their real (older) dates. That unlocks the Past Lives trophy.

Hour meters

Shadowing (≈50, with your DO hours and number of physicians), Clinical (≈300), Non-clinical service (≈150), Research (≈100), Leadership (≈100), and a running total for Work. A ⭐ appears when you pass a goal. These are common advisor targets, not rules.

Delete an entry: 🗑 then Sure?. There's no edit, so delete and re-log to fix one. 📋 Copy for Sheets/Excel and ⬇ Download CSV export the whole journal.

📘 Courses and GPA (Spellbook)

Prereqs come preloaded and marked ⭐. For each course, set Status (Not yet / In progress / ✓ Done), Grade (picking a grade marks it done), Credits and School. Med schools count every college course, including Lone Star, so add those too.

  • ➕ Add another course: name, type (Non-science, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Math), credits, school.
  • Tiles: Cumulative GPA (est.), BCPM science GPA (est.) and Prereqs complete.
  • Copy or download your courses for Sheets.

AMCAS and AACOMAS use their own grade conversions, so these are estimates.

💾 How saving works

Everything saves automatically. There's no save button. Progress lives in up to three places, and the save status in the top bar tells you which:

Status saysMeaning
Saving in this browserJust loaded and still checking for an account (about a second).
Saved on this device · log in to the Village to back it upOnly this browser has your progress. Log in to the Village.
☁ Saved to Bailey's Village accountBacked up. 🎉
☁ Loaded newer progress from your other deviceAnother device saved more recently, so this one caught up.

1. This browser

Instant, offline. Lost if browser data is cleared, in a private tab, in another browser or on another phone. Safari can also clear data for sites you haven't visited in a while.

2. Your Village account

Logged in to the Family Village in the same browser? The game saves there about a second after each change. It saves to whoever is logged in to the Village in that browser, and the status shows that person's name.

  1. Go to bailsformed.github.io/village/ in the browser you play in.
  2. 👋 I've been here before → family code, name, PIN → Let me in.
  3. Reload MedQuest and check the status shows ☁.
📱 iPhone Home Screen icons keep their own storage, separate from Safari. Log in to the Village wherever you actually open the game.

3. Backups

The Sage reminds you weekly. In ⚙︎ → 💾 Weekly backup:

  • ⬇ Download backup file: a medquest-backup-….json file. Keep it in your Med School folder.
  • 📋 Copy backup code: one long line of text to paste into a doc or email. If copying is blocked, a box shows the code to copy by hand.
  • Restore: paste a code in the box and load it, or pick a backup file.
Restoring replaces everything, and the restored version becomes your Village copy too. Reset all progress wipes this device and your Village copy, so copy a backup code first.

Multiple devices

After a device has connected once, newest save wins. The first time a device connects, the bigger save wins, so a blank new phone can't overwrite real progress.

📱 New phone

  1. Old phone: open MedQuest once while it shows ☁, so the latest progress is uploaded. Copy a backup code too, for safety.
  2. New phone: log in to the Village (family code, name, PIN).
  3. Same browser: open bailsformed.github.io/medquest/. Your save loads. Check for ☁ in the status.
  4. No Village? Paste your backup code in ⚙︎ and restore.

🦉 Tips from the Sage

  • Log the same day, with a contact name and one line of reflection. Those reflections become your activity descriptions.
  • Backfill past jobs first. Instant XP, a trophy, and it reminds you how much experience you already have.
  • Keep the Spellbook honest, including withdrawals and old Lone Star grades. AMCAS will see them anyway.
  • Use quest notes for advisor names, emails and what was said in meetings.
  • Copy the Journal to Sheets each semester as an extra backup.
  • Face the Fork honestly. A gap year isn't losing, it's strategy.

❓ FAQ and fixes

My progress disappeared.

Don't log anything new yet. Log in to the Village in this browser and reload. If that wasn't set up, restore your latest backup from ⚙︎.

Status says "Saved on this device" and never shows ☁.

No Village login in this browser (or you're in a private tab). Log in to the Village in the same browser, then reload.

The status shows someone else's name.

Someone else is logged in to the Village in this browser, so the game is saving to their account. In the Village, open your profile, scroll to the bottom and tap 🚪 Log out on this device, log in as yourself, and reload.

Wrong hours in an entry.

Delete it (🗑 → Sure?) and log it again.

Downloads don't work.

Some browsers block downloads from web pages. Use the Copy buttons instead and paste into Sheets, a doc or an email.

Can family see my hours?

Not other Village residents. The save is stored under your account and isn't shown anywhere in the Village.
