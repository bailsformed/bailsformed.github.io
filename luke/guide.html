01 · What this operation is

You're a sophomore biology major at LSU with three possible paths in front of you: ✚ MEDICAL, ⚖ LEGAL and ? WILDCARD (anything else a bio degree can lead to). Open Field is a two-and-a-half-year campaign that helps you scout all three and figure out which one is actually yours.

It does that three ways:

  • Missions tell you the next real thing to do (talk to an advisor, shadow a doctor, sit in on court) and why it matters.
  • After-action reports record how each thing made you feel. Over time the game turns them into a signal showing which path keeps lighting you up.
  • Logs keep your hours and classes, which med and law schools both ask for later.

No path is the "right" answer. The game doesn't push you anywhere. It just shows you your own data.

Course numbers and dates are LSU's usual patterns and estimates. Confirm with your advisors, the AAMC (med) and LSAC (law).

03 · Every tab

HQ

  • The operations map. Tap a zone: HQ · Missions, Range · Training, Tower · Field Log, Bunker · Intel jump to those tabs. Med Outpost, Courthouse and Unknown Territory open a path dossier: what that career is like, your signal for it, and its missions.
  • Current objective: your phase, your next mission, and ▶ Accept mission.
  • Signal strength: a mini version of the Intel Board.
  • Timeline: finals, semester starts, THE CALL, application windows, graduation. "≈" means estimated.
  • The map says something on it is hidden. It isn't lying. See Hidden intel.

Missions

All 38 missions, grouped into 7 phases, with a progress bar per phase and ● ACTIVE on your current one. Tap any open mission to read its brief. Details in Missions and THE CALL.

Debrief

  • Intel Board: signal bars for Medical, Legal and Wildcard, with average energy and curiosity, plus an ANALYSIS line.
  • + File After-Action Report: debrief anything, any time (a class, a shadow day, an event).
  • Values recon: pick your top 5 career values.
  • Report archive: every report you've filed, newest first. Delete one with Delete → Confirm. Copy for Sheets / Download export them.

Field Log

Your hours. Tiles show totals per type. Fill in the form and hit Log it:

  • Type: Shadowing · MD, Shadowing · DO, Clinical (patient contact), Legal exposure (court / law office), Community service, Research, Leadership, Work / job.
  • Date, Hours (quarter-hours work, like 2.75), Where, Contact (who can vouch for it) and a Note.

Entries show in a table below. To delete one, hit ✕ then Sure?. There's no edit, so delete and re-log to fix a mistake. Copy for Sheets pastes into Google Sheets or Excel. Download CSV saves a file.

Rough targets: med schools like about 40–60 shadowing hours with several doctors, plus steady clinical hours. Law schools care more about depth and leadership than hour counts.

Training

Your class record, preloaded with LSU's usual pre-med set (Orgo I and Microbiology start as In progress).

  • Change Cr (credits), Status (Not yet / In progress / ✓ Done) and Grade right in the table. Picking a grade marks the class Done.
  • Add a class at the bottom: name, type (Non-science, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Math) and credits. Classes you add get a ✕ to remove them.
  • Tiles show Cumulative GPA, Science GPA (BIOL/CHEM/PHYS/MATH only) and Pre-med core done. Both GPAs are estimates. W (withdrew) doesn't count toward them.

Intel

  • Intel caches: 12 hidden finds, each a short true story from medicine, law or science, plus a "dig deeper" question.
  • Field radio: type a frequency and hit Tune.
  • Rabbit holes: log anything you wondered about. Pick a tag (Science, Law, People, History, Other) and tick I looked it up if you did. +15 XP each.

Loadout

  • Change your callsign, hair, skin and jacket.
  • Gear & commendations: items you unlock (list in XP, rank and gear).
  • Campaign ribbons: one lights up for each phase you finish.

04 · Missions and THE CALL

Running a mission

  1. Open it from HQ, the Missions tab, or a path dossier.
  2. Read the brief and why it matters.
  3. Tap each objective as you do it (+10 XP). Tap again to uncheck.
  4. Read Command's note. Use Mission notes for names, dates and what you noticed. They save as you type.
  5. Hit Mission complete (+100 XP). The after-action report opens right away.

You don't need every objective checked to complete a mission. Done by mistake? Open it and hit Mark incomplete. Finished missions also have a File debrief button if you want to report on them again later.

Path missions

Some missions carry a path tag (MEDICAL LEGAL WILDCARD RECON).

  • Phases 1–4: every path mission is open to you. Scouting all of them is the point.
  • Phases 5–7: path missions show 🔒 unlocks after THE CALL.

THE CALL

THE CALL is the first mission of Phase 5 (around spring 2028). Review your Intel Board, talk it through with someone you trust, then pick one: ✚ MEDICAL, ⚖ LEGAL, ? WILDCARD, or 🔭 EXTEND RECON (a gap year or more time to decide). You have to pick before Command lets you complete it.

After the call, only your path's missions show in Phases 5–7. The others disappear. Your soldier gets path gear too.

Not a forever choice. Open THE CALL again and tap a different path any time. The missions switch to match.

05 · Debriefs and signal strength

Filing an after-action report (+25 XP)

  • Energy after: 🪫 Drained, ➖ Neutral or ⚡ Charged.
  • Curiosity: 1–5, how much you wanted to know more.
  • Which direction did this point you? Medical, Legal, Wildcard or ○ Nowhere. (A path mission pre-fills its own path. Change it if that's not how it felt.)
  • The question: every mission asks its own honest question. Free-form reports ask "What did you notice about yourself?"

How the signal works

Each report adds energy × curiosity to the path it pointed toward. A Charged (3) report with curiosity 5 adds 15. A Drained (1) report with curiosity 1 adds 1. Reports pointed "Nowhere" don't count toward any path.

Values recon adds to the signal too. Pick 5 of 16 values (like "Solving puzzles & diagnosing problems" or "Arguing a point / debating"), hit Lock it in (+50 XP, once). Each value leans toward some paths more than others. You can re-run it any time.

The bars compare paths to each other: the leading path is always a full bar. The ANALYSIS line reads:

  • Fewer than 3 reports: Not enough data yet.
  • Top two within about 15%: Close race. Run more recon on both.
  • Otherwise: Strongest signal, the path leading on energy × curiosity.

Data, not destiny. It's a mirror, not an order.

07 · Hidden intel

There are 12 intel caches. Each locked one on the Intel tab tells you how it's found (map, a frequency, a code, and so on). Recover all 12 to open the 📁 CLASSIFIED FILE. It's worth it.

Try on your own first. If you're stuck, open the hints one level at a time.

🔎 Hints (no answers)
  • On the HQ map (5): look for tiny things that aren't buildings. They're small, so zoom in or go slow. One has a note that starts the radio trail.
  • Radio frequencies (3): each one's "Transmission" gives you a history clue. The answer is always a year.
  • Classic code: an old video-game cheat code works on a keyboard. On a phone, something in the top bar responds to being tapped a lot, fast.
  • Annoy Command: Command's face on HQ doesn't love being poked.
  • 5 debriefs: exactly what it says.
  • After midnight: exactly what it says.
🚨 Full answers (spoilers)
  • Map: a flickering bulb near the Bunker, a supply crate at the upper left, dog tags on a fence at the bottom right, an abandoned radio near the Tower, and a strange triangle symbol in the bottom-left corner. Tap each one.
  • Radio: tune 1854, then 1963, then 1966.
  • Classic code: ↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A on a keyboard, or tap your rank (the word next to your callsign) 7 times quickly.
  • Command: tap Command's face in the HQ objective panel 5 times.
  • Debriefs: file 5 after-action reports.
  • Night watch: open the game or make any change between midnight and 4 a.m.

08 · Securing your data

No save button. The game saves every change automatically. Your progress lives in up to three places:

1. This browser (always on)

Instant and works offline. Lost if you clear your browser history or website data, use a private tab, switch browsers or phones. Safari can also clear data for sites you haven't opened in a while.

2. Your Village account (automatic backup)

Logged in to the Family Village in the same browser? Then the game also saves to your Village account about a second after every change.

  • First time it connects: ☁ PROGRESS NOW BACKED UP TO YOUR VILLAGE ACCOUNT.
  • The footer at the very bottom then reads SAVES ON THIS DEVICE + LUKE'S VILLAGE ACCOUNT. Just SAVES ON THIS DEVICE means you're not connected.
It saves to whoever is logged in to the Village in that browser. Play while logged in as you.

Logging in to the Village

  1. Same browser you play in: go to bailsformed.github.io/village/.
  2. Tap 👋 I've been here before. (Never joined? Use the invite link Bailey sent, or 🔑 I'm new here.)
  3. Enter the family code, your name and your 4-digit PIN → Let me in.
  4. Back in the game, reload. Check the footer mentions your Village account.
📱 iPhone Home Screen icons keep their own storage, separate from Safari. Log in to the Village in the same place you open the game.

3. Backup codes

Once a week a ⚠ SECURE YOUR DATA bar shows up. Hit Back up (or Tomorrow to snooze a day). Or go to ⚙ SYS any time:

  • ⬇ Download backup: saves a file like open-field-backup-2026-11-13.json.
  • Copy backup code: copies one long line of text. Paste it into Notes or email it to yourself. If copying is blocked, a box shows the code: select it, copy it, hit ✓ I copied it.
  • Restore: choose the file under Restore from file, or paste the code and hit Restore. You'll see SAVE RESTORED.
Restoring replaces everything with the backup, including your Village copy. Reset everything wipes this device and your Village copy, so back up first.

Two devices

Once a device has connected, the newest save wins, so a phone and a laptop stay in sync. The very first time a device connects, the bigger save wins, so a fresh, empty phone can't erase your campaign.

10 · Field tips

  • File a report the same day you do something. Your gut reaction is the most useful data, and it fades fast.
  • Use ○ Nowhere honestly. Lots of things won't point anywhere. That's real data too.
  • Debrief more than missions. A class that fired you up, a lecture that drained you, a conversation that stuck. Use + File After-Action Report.
  • Log hours with a contact name. Applications ask who can verify them.
  • Use Rabbit Holes for every random question. Five of them get you the Flashlight.
  • Before THE CALL, export your debriefs to Sheets and read them start to finish.
  • Make a backup code monthly and keep it in your email.

11 · Troubleshooting

Everything's gone. Rank is back to Recruit.

Don't log anything new yet. You're probably in a different browser or a private tab, or the browser data got cleared. Log in to the Village in this browser and reload the game. If that's not set up, restore your latest backup from ⚙ SYS. Still nothing? Text Bailey.

The footer only says "SAVES ON THIS DEVICE".

No Village login found in this browser. Log in to the Village here, then reload. Check the Home Screen icon note in Securing your data.

Missions are locked with 🔒.

Those are path missions in Phases 5–7. They unlock once you complete THE CALL, and then only your chosen path's missions show.

I picked the wrong path at THE CALL.

Open THE CALL (Missions tab, Phase 5) and tap a different path. Missions update right away.

The signal bars barely move.

It needs at least 3 reports to say anything, and it compares paths against each other. File reports on things in every path, not just one.

I typed a frequency and got static.

Wrong frequency. Every radio code is a 4-digit year. Read the "Transmission" at the bottom of the cache that sent you there.

Can I fix a log entry or a report?

No edit button. Delete it (two taps) and add it again.

Can my family see my debriefs?

Not other Village residents. Your game isn't shown anywhere in the Village. It's stored under your own account, and only your login loads it.
