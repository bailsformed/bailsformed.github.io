📜 What the dungeon is for

Under the jokes, this is a real planner for getting into med school from SUNY New Paltz: eight floors, about two and a half years, one white coat.

  • 53 quests explain each real step (advisors, AAMC ID, prereqs, shadowing, MCAT, AMCAS and AACOMAS, interviews), why it matters, and exactly what to do.
  • The Grind Log is your record of every hour (with contact names) for applications.
  • The Skill Sheet tracks every course and estimates your GPA.
  • The Loot Ledger connects to the money Pot you share with Bailey.

The planned route is Straight Through: apply May 2028, start med school in 2029. A checkpoint in Jan 2028 (The Fork in the Road) lets you choose a gap year instead.

Dates marked ≈ are estimates. Confirm on the official AAMC, AACOM and New Paltz sites and with your advisor.

🦦 Who's who

  • 📢 The System: runs the dungeon. Rude. Gives achievements. Is not your mom.
  • Lady Bailsworth, your stoat companion (bottom of the top bar). Complains constantly, supervises everything, takes partial credit for your level-ups. Tap her to pet her. You can rename her in ⚙︎.
  • Archivist Tobble, a toad in a hat who keeps the Guild Archive and actually explains things.
  • You, the Crawler. Tap your portrait to change your name and colors.
🦦 Lady Bailsworth: I'd like it noted that I commissioned this entire dungeon. For you. You're welcome. Also I'm hungry.

Inside a quest

  • A System announcement, a bit of stoat banter, then what the quest is and why it matters.
  • ✦ Objectives: tap each one as you do it (+10 XP). Tap again to uncheck.
  • Tobble's tip, useful links, and a notes box (saves as you type).
  • Complete it for the quest's XP (40 to 300). Undo with Mark not done.

Clearing every quest on a floor gets you a FLOOR CLEARED announcement. Lady Bailsworth will demand to be carried down the stairs.

Special quests

  • Form a party (Floor 1): you and Bailey are on the exact same route. Set a weekly study date and quiz each other.

📓 Grind Log

Fill in ➕ Log the grind and tap Log it (+2 XP per hour):

  • Date, Hours, Type (Shadowing · MD, Shadowing · DO, Clinical · Paid job, Clinical · Volunteer, Non-clinical service, Research, Leadership, Work).
  • Place / organization, Contact (name / email), and what happened / what you learned.

Always fill in Contact for shadowing. The Serial Shadower achievement counts different contact names. Log your pre-school jobs with their real older dates for the Ancient One achievement.

The grind meters show progress toward common targets: shadowing ≈50, clinical ≈300, service ≈150, research ≈100, leadership ≈100. Targets, not rules.

Delete an entry with 🗑 → Sure? (no edit, so delete and re-log to fix). 📋 Copy for Sheets/Excel and ⬇ Download CSV export everything.

🧪 Skill Sheet

Prereqs come preloaded. For each course set Status, Grade (picking one marks it done), Credits and School. Med schools count every course you've ever taken, at every school, so add them all with ➕ Add another course.

Tiles show Cumulative GPA, BCPM science GPA (Bio, Chem, Phys, Math) and prereqs complete. Estimates only, since AMCAS and AACOMAS convert grades their own way.

Loot boxes

Every achievement drops a loot box: BRONZE SILVER GOLD LEGENDARY. Tap a box, tap Open it!, and a random (useless, beautiful) item goes into your 🎒 Inventory. Rarer achievements give rarer boxes.

📢 SYSTEM: Loot boxes contain no real-world value. Neither does your sense of time during orgo.

💰 Loot Ledger (money)

The Loot Ledger is the dungeon-skinned version of the Pot, the money tracker you share with Bailey. Same numbers Bailey sees. It's separate from the Village and uses its own email and password.

Signing in

  1. Open the 💰 Loot Ledger tab and tap Sign in.
  2. Enter your Pot email and password (8+ characters). Forgot it? Tap Forgot password.
  3. You stay signed in on that browser.

What's on the page

  • Just me / Both of us switches between your money and the shared view. ‹ › changes the month.
  • Tiles: what's in the pot right now (both of you, cash minus card debt), your cash, and how much you've spent this month against the budget.
  • 📥 Upload bank scroll: import a bank statement file (CSV). The System sorts it, ignores duplicates, and only asks about purchases it can't figure out.
  • + Log money: add one expense, income or transfer by hand.
  • 🏦 Balances, 🧾 Budget and 📅 Bills: account balances, monthly budgets per category, and recurring bills.
  • Budget jars are grouped as 💀 Mandatory Boss Fights (must-pay), 🎒 Party Supplies (everyday) and 🎁 Loot Box Impulses (fun money). Tap a jar to log spending in it.
  • Entries: this month's transactions. Delete one with two taps.

💀 Boss fights

Bills due in the next 10 days show up as bosses: 👹 upcoming, 🔥 ENRAGED (late), ☠️ defeated (paid). Tap Defeat when you've paid one. Tap Undo if you tapped it by mistake. Bills on autopay say so.

The 🍯 Open the joint Pot ↗ button opens the full Pot site in a new tab.

💾 Don't let the dungeon eat your save

There's no save button. Every change saves by itself, in up to three places. The save status next to ⚙︎ tells you which:

StatusMeaning
Saved on this deviceOnly this browser has it (or it's still checking for a login).
Saved on this device · log in to the Village to back it upNo login found. Not backed up.
☁ Saved to your Pot loginBacked up to your Pot account. 🎉
☁ Saved to [name]'s Village accountBacked up to a Family Village account. 🎉
☁ Loaded newer progress from your other deviceAnother device saved more recently, so this one caught up.

1. This browser

Instant, offline, and fragile. It's lost if browser data is cleared, in a private tab, in another browser, or on a new phone. Safari can also tidy away data for sites you haven't opened in a while.

2. Your account (the safety net)

The dungeon backs itself up to an account logged in in the same browser. It checks in this order:

  1. Your Pot login (the Loot Ledger sign-in). If you're signed in to the Ledger, you're covered.
  2. A Family Village account, if you have one: go to bailsformed.github.io/village/ → 👋 I've been here before → family code, name, PIN.

After signing in, reload the dungeon and check the status says ☁.

It saves to whoever is logged in in that browser. If the status shows someone else's name, you're saving to their account. Sign out of theirs and into yours.
📱 iPhone Home Screen icons keep their own storage, separate from Safari. Sign in wherever you actually open the dungeon.

3. Backups

Lady Bailsworth nags you weekly ("If the Dungeon eats our data I'm going to scream"). In ⚙︎:

  • ⬇ Download backup file: a .json file with everything (hours, courses, quests, notes and loot).
  • 📋 Copy backup code: one long line of text. Paste it into Notes or email it to yourself.
  • Restore: paste a code or pick a backup file.
Restoring replaces everything, including your account copy. Reset wipes this device and your account copy. Back up first.

Two devices

Once a device has connected, newest save wins. The first time a device connects, the bigger save wins, so a blank new phone can't wipe a real run.

📱 New phone

  1. Old phone: open the dungeon once while the status shows ☁. Copy a backup code too, just in case.
  2. New phone: open the dungeon, go to 💰 Loot Ledger and sign in to the Pot (or log in to the Village).
  3. Reload the dungeon. Your run loads from your account. Check for ☁.
  4. No account? ⚙︎ → paste your backup code → restore.

🗡️ Survival tips

  • Log the same day, with a contact name and a line about what you learned. Applications ask for all three.
  • Backfill old jobs first. Free XP and a loot box.
  • Open loot boxes right away. The System gets passive-aggressive otherwise. (It doesn't. But it could.)
  • Use quest notes for advisor names, emails and deadlines.
  • Defeat bosses the day you pay them so the Ledger matches reality.
  • Pet the stoat. No mechanical benefit. Emotional benefit: significant.

❓ FAQ

My whole run is gone.

Don't log anything yet. Sign in to the Loot Ledger (or the Village) in this browser and reload. If neither was set up, restore your latest backup from ⚙︎. Then tell Bailey.

The status never shows ☁.

No Pot or Village login in this browser, or you're in a private tab. Sign in to the Loot Ledger, then reload.

The Loot Ledger says to sign in, but I'm logged in to the Village.

They're separate. The Ledger only uses your Pot email and password.

I marked a bill as defeated by mistake.

Tap Undo next to it.

Wrong hours in a Grind Log entry.

Delete it (🗑 → Sure?) and log it again.

Can anyone else see my quests and hours?

No one else in the Village. Your save is stored under your own login. (The Ledger is different: that money is shared with Bailey by design.)
