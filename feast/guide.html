What the café is

When work is done, you turn down a side street and find a little Paris café. Warm light, jazz, one empty chair with a blank brass plate. That's your seat. Come by any evening.

Out there, you do grown-up things. In here, the café notices:

  • Billable hours fill the bookshelf (one book per 5 hours).
  • Bills you've paid light candles on your table.
  • Savings drop coins in the jar on the bar (one coin per month of must-pay bills covered).
  • Taking care of yourself keeps the little stove warm.

Madame Cendre, the cat, runs the place. Tap her for another line. Famous people from old Paris sometimes sit at your table with a real quote.

Money, tax and loan facts in the game are general education, not personal advice. Check big decisions with the official source it links to.

✨ One thing & 🌧 rough day

✨ Just do this one thing

It's at the top of every screen. Tap it and you get one task, chosen in this order:

  1. A reminder that's due (a bill, taxes, a Friday time check).
  2. A setup step, if one is missing (read a paycheck, set a budget, add loans, start savings).
  3. The next step of your next quest.

Buttons on the card:

  • Not this one shows a different one.
  • That's enough for today closes it. That's a real answer, not a failure.
  • For bills: Paid ✓ or Tomorrow.

🌧 Rough day mode

Tap 🌧 Rough day at the top. Tap it again to turn it off. While it's on:

  • The Café says "Nothing is required."
  • All Quiet shows only bills and taxes.
  • Reminders show only must-pay things due in the last few days. No "next 30 days" list.
  • The one-thing button only offers what's actually due. If nothing is, it says so.

The next day, Cendre asks how you are. Pick Still rough or A little better.

On the Café, 🌧 Hard day? Sit with Cendre gives you a short breathing exercise and just one next step. You can turn on rough day mode from there too.

If it's heavier than a rough day, call or text 988, any time. The "Know your lifelines" quest has the Florida Bar's confidential helpline too.

☕ Café

Home. You'll see:

  • The café scene: bookshelf, candles, coin jar, stove, the moon over Paris.
  • Madame Cendre. Tap her for another line.
  • Next on the Path, with Open the quest.
  • Quick things: ⏱ Log time, 💶 Log spending, 🔥 Hearth check-in.
  • 🪑 Your table: today's guest and their quote. Invite someone to sit brings another (+10 XP for each new guest).

The top bar has your picture and level, 🎷 (jazz on or off) and ⚙ Settings. If a timer is running, a ⏱ chip shows the time. Tap it to jump to Hours.

🕊️ All Quiet

An honest check-up. It lists what needs you first, then says "Everything else is quiet." It checks:

🧾 paycheck read🕯️ bills paid🍷 over budget🛟 savings cushion🎓 loans🧾 taxes (in season)🔥 stove⏱ last time logged📜 CLE🗝️ next quest💾 backup

  • ✓ Check means "I've looked at this." It remembers, and won't nag you again until something changes.
  • Open takes you to the right screen.

🔔 Reminders

  • Today: what's due now. Tap Paid ✓, Done ✓, or Tomorrow.
  • Next 30 days: what's coming.
  • Automatic ones: bill and loan due days, card payments, a monthly 10-minute "money date", Friday "is this week's time logged?", tax season, credit report peeks, and CLE dates.
  • 📌 Add your own: a title, a date, and once, monthly or yearly.
  • 📲 Add to my phone calendar downloads a calendar file. Open it on your phone and tap Add All.

⏱ Hours

The timer

  1. Type the Client, Matter and What you're doing (all optional).
  2. Tap ▶ Start timer.
  3. Tap ■ Stop & save when you're done. Discard throws it away (tap twice).

Time rounds up to the next tenth of an hour (0.1 = 6 minutes), like most firms bill. The timer keeps running if you close the page. If it's been going over 4 hours, the one-thing button checks with you.

✍️ Quick entry

Forgot the timer? Add the date, hours (like 0.3), client, matter and a description. Tip: verb + what + why.

📚 The shelf

  • Totals for today, this week, this month and this year.
  • Set a yearly target (like 1800) to see a weekly goal and your pace.
  • Each client gets a shelf of books.
  • Delete an entry: ✕, then tap again. 📋 Copy for Sheets or ⬇ CSV to export.

📜 CLE tracker

Florida asks for 33 hours every 3 years, including 5 ethics-related and 3 technology. Add your bar admission date, your reporting date, and each course. Tick Practicing with Professionalism course done when it is. Your Florida Bar portal is the official record.

📅 Google Calendar

Turns meetings into time entries. It only reads your calendar and never changes anything.

  1. On Hours, tap 📅 Connect Google Calendar.
  2. The first time, it asks for a Client ID (one-time setup). Ask Bails for it. Bails also adds your Gmail as a tester. Paste it in and tap Save & connect.
  3. Sign in with Google and allow read-only access.
  4. Pick dates and a calendar, tap Show events, tick the meetings that were work, then tap Import checked.

Imported entries show 📅 in the table. The Google connection lasts for this browser session, so you may need to tap Connect again another day.

💶 Treasury

Your money, one month at a time. ‹ › changes the month.

Start with two things

  1. 🧾 Read my paycheck: copy each line from one pay stub. Cendre checks the math and works out your monthly take-home pay.
  2. Budget & bill dates: rough monthly amounts per category, plus due days for bills. Guesses are fine.

What's on the page

  • Tiles: take-home per month, bills paid (a candle each), savings cushion (a coin each), spent vs. planned.
  • Three groups of jars: 🕯️ Le Nécessaire (must-pay), 🥖 Le Quotidien (everyday), 🍷 Les Plaisirs (fun money). Tap a jar to see entries, change its amount or due day, or 🕯️ Mark paid this month. A must-pay bill also counts as paid once you've logged about its full amount.
  • 🛟 Savings: an emergency fund (first stop $1,000, then 3 months of must-pay) and a someday jar for joy, with no deadline, ever.
  • 📈 Credit: card names and balances (never card numbers), how much of your limits you're using, and your credit score when you check it.
  • This month's entries, with Copy for Sheets and ⬇ CSV.

Adding money

  • + Log money: something spent, something that came in, or a transfer.
  • 📥 Import a statement: download a CSV from your bank (Activity or Statements → Download → CSV), pick which account it's from, and upload or paste it. Cendre sorts what's obvious and asks only about the unclear ones. Duplicates are skipped, so uploading twice is harmless. She remembers your answers next time.

🎓 Loans

  • + Add a loan: name, federal or private, balance, interest rate, monthly payment, due day. StudentAid.gov lists your federal loans.
  • Each loan shows its payoff time and total interest. Tap Paid ✓ each month.
  • My repayment plan: pick yours from the list.
  • 🧮 What if I paid a little extra? Slide to see how much sooner you'd be done.
  • Short explainers on plans, PSLF and refinancing. Rules change, so always check StudentAid.gov.

🔥 Hearth

A quick self-care check-in. Tap what you did today:

💧 Water🍽️ A real meal😴 7+ hrs sleep🏋️ Moved🌤️ Outside💬 Talked to someone💊 Meds / vitamins🎧 Music📖 Read for fun🧒 Family time🛋️ Real rest

  • Six things make a full stove. Honest, not perfect.
  • Warmth only counts the days you checked in. Missed days never count against you.
  • Add a note if you like. Change the date to fill in a day you missed.

⚙ Settings

Tap ⚙ Settings or your picture.

  • Reading: Standard font or Easy-read font (Lexend, wider spacing), and three text sizes (A A A).
  • Your look: name, outfit, hair style and color, skin, glasses, stubble.
  • The cat's name and Soft sounds.
  • Exports: every list as Copy or ⬇ CSV.
  • Saving & backup (see below).
  • Sorting rules I've learned from imports. ✕ removes one that sorts things wrong.
  • Google Calendar: forget the saved Client ID.
  • Replay the welcome and Erase everything.

💾 Saving

No save button. Everything saves by itself. The save line near the top of the screen tells you where:

☁ Loaded newer progress from your other deviceYour other phone or laptop saved more recently, so this one caught up.

Log in to the Village

  1. In the same browser you use for the café, go to bailsformed.github.io/village/.
  2. Tap 👋 I've been here before (or use the invite link Bails sent you).
  3. Enter the family code, your name and your PIN, then tap Let me in.
  4. Reload the café. The line should start with ☁.
📱 On iPhone, a Home Screen icon keeps its own storage, separate from Safari. Log in to the Village in the same place you open the café.
It saves to whoever is logged in to the Village in that browser. If you see someone else's name, log them out first.

Backup codes

In ⚙ Settings → 💾 Saving & backup:

  • ⬇ Download backup: a file named like moveable-feast-backup-2026-11-13.json.
  • Copy backup code: one long line of text. Paste it into Notes or email it to yourself. If copying is blocked, a box shows the code to copy by hand.
  • Restore: pick the file, or paste the code and tap Restore.
Restoring replaces everything, including your Village copy. Erase everything wipes this device and your Village copy (it keeps your look and reading settings). Make a backup first.

Two devices? After a device has connected once, the newest save wins. The very first time, the bigger save wins, so an empty new phone can't wipe your café.

📱 New phone

  1. Old phone: open the café once while the line shows ☁. Copy a backup code too, just in case.
  2. New phone: log in to the Village (family code, name, PIN).
  3. Same browser: open bailsformed.github.io/feast/. Your café comes back.
  4. No Village? ⚙ Settings → paste your backup code → Restore.

The Google Calendar Client ID is part of your save, so it comes along too. Just tap Connect again.

❓ Help

Everything's gone.

Don't add anything new yet. Log in to the Village in this browser and reload. If that wasn't set up, restore your latest backup from ⚙ Settings. Then tell Bails.

The save line never shows ☁.

No Village login in this browser, or you're in a private tab. Log in to the Village here, then reload.

I forgot to stop the timer.

Tap Discard (twice), then add the real time with Quick entry.

Google says it isn't connected.

The connection only lasts for a browser session. Tap Connect Google Calendar again.

An import sorted something wrong.

Delete that entry in Treasury and log it again. Then remove the rule in ⚙ Settings → Sorting rules I've learned.

I missed a week.

Nothing is lost and nothing resets. Tap ✨ and do one thing. Cendre will pretend she didn't notice.

Can my family see my money or hours?

No. The café isn't linked anywhere on the site or shown anywhere in the Village. Your save is stored under your own login.
